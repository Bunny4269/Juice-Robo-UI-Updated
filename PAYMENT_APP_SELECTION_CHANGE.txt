JUICE ROBO — PAYMENT APP SELECTION CHANGE

Changed:
- PaymentScreen no longer shows Cards, Netbanking, Wallets, or generic payment-method selection.
- Proceed to Payment now opens a UPI-only app selection screen.
- Only PhonePe and Google Pay are offered.
- Selecting an app creates the backend order/payment session and launches the selected UPI app.
- Google Pay uses gpay://upi/pay on iOS and tez://upi/pay on Android.
- PhonePe uses phonepe://upi/pay.
- Existing VITE_JR_UPI_ID configuration remains the source for the UPI payee when a payment URL must be constructed.
- Backend payment verification remains authoritative; launching a UPI app never marks payment successful.
- PaymentPendingScreen also offers only PhonePe and Google Pay for reopening the payment app.
- No backend files were modified.
